# How to Rank #1 in ChatGPT: Your Guide to AEO

**Source:** GTM Strategist · Maja Voje & George Chasiotis · May 29, 2026

## Core Thesis

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the next frontier of inbound — and 86% of organizations are either unprepared or measuring it wrong. Only 14% of 599 marketers surveyed feel confident in their AEO strategy. AI assistants are now a primary discovery layer for B2B buyers, and most companies aren't showing up.

## The Six Pillars of AI Search Visibility

### 1. Topic Breadth and Depth
Cover related topics authentically within your domain. Off-topic content expansion backfires — Connecteam's 500+ competitor review pages caused both organic and AI citation declines after Google's December 2025 update.

### 2. Publishing Velocity
AI assistants favor newer content — average cited content is 1,064 days old vs. Google's 1,432 days. Freshness matters, but updating existing high-value pieces is as important as publishing new ones.

### 3. Content Optimization for Extraction
Clear introductions, logical heading hierarchies, succinct paragraphs, and strategic tables help AI systems extract relevant passages. Structure for the machine, not just the human reader.

### 4. Crawl Efficiency
- Verify AI crawlers aren't blocked in `robots.txt`
- Create an `llms.txt` or AI Info page with brand disambiguation and target audience specs
- Use clean, semantic URL architecture

### 5. Content Monitoring
Build a signal-based refresh system: light refreshes (<30% changes), content updates (>30%), or full rewrites. Automate flagging; keep humans in QA loops.

### 6. Brand Experience and Perception
Move beyond backlinks. Owned perception (directories, positioning pages, AI Info pages), multi-channel presence (YouTube gets 34% more AI Overview citations), and authentic community engagement all drive AI visibility.

## Measurement Framework

40.6% of teams struggle with AEO measurement and attribution. Track across:
- **Referral traffic** from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude
- **Citation share** and visibility metrics
- **Sentiment** of how your brand appears in AI responses
- **AI crawler activity** and Overview impressions

Use both topical prompts (general use-case questions) and evaluation prompts (testing brand perception directly). Triangulate across GA4, Search Console, server logs, and AI tracking tools — no single platform is complete.

## Key Stats
- 47.2% of companies report declining organic traffic; 26.7% dropped >20%
- Only 7.7% have dedicated AEO teams despite 82% claiming some adoption
- AI search favors content under 3 years old
- YouTube gets 34% more citations in AI Overviews than text-only content

## RevOps Implications

As AI-referred buyers arrive pre-educated and with stronger intent (per Adobe data), AEO directly affects pipeline quality — not just traffic volume. Lead scoring models that ignore AI-referred traffic source are missing a high-intent signal. The companies that rank in AI search will see better MQL-to-SQL conversion because buyers arrive already sold on the category.
