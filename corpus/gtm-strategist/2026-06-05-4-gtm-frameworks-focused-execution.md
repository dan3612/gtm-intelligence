# 4 GTM Frameworks for Focused Execution

**Source:** GTM Strategist · Maja Voje · June 5, 2026

## Overview

Four go-to-market frameworks tested across 12,000+ companies over 15 years. Each is workshop-based and designed to move teams from strategic confusion to executable plans within 3-6 month timeframes.

> "Your edge isn't more output. It's focus and proprietary insight."

Unclear ICP and positioning issues affect ~90% of new products. If your GTM plan reaches much further than six months out, it's probably fiction.

---

## Framework 1: GTM Power Hour

**Purpose:** Create a foundational GTM plan in 60 minutes.

Maps the complete go-to-market motion and produces a shareable v1 plan. Ideal for founders with analysis paralysis who need direction, not more research recommendations.

**Planning horizon rule:** Limit to 3-6 months maximum. Beyond that, plans become fiction.

---

## Framework 2: Market Problem Map

**Purpose:** Identify and validate a beachhead segment instead of serving everyone.

Spray-and-pray approaches dilute resources and prevent accumulation of proof that the solution works in any single segment.

**Beachhead segment criteria:**
1. Pressing, high-pain need for the solution
2. Adequate budget and willingness to pay
3. Ability to generate case studies and references
4. Realistic accessibility from current position

**Scoring method:** Test multiple ICP hypotheses across three dimensions:
- Pain level
- Ease of reach
- Implementation simplicity

Identify the best initial market by scoring all three, not just one.

---

## Framework 3: Differentiated Positioning Workflow

**Purpose:** Move beyond generic "vanilla AI tool" messaging to distinctive competitive positioning.

**Five-stage process:**
1. Map competitive alternatives currently used to solve the problem
2. Score ICP satisfaction with existing solutions
3. Identify genuine competitive assets (exclude generic claims)
4. Translate differentiators into benefits supported by proof points
5. Develop positioning statement: value proposition + three benefits

**Key insight:** Proof points are increasingly non-negotiable given buyer skepticism toward AI marketing claims. Any differentiation claim without proof is noise.

---

## Framework 4: GTM Blueprint

**Purpose:** Reverse-engineer a prioritized quarterly execution plan from a measurable objective.

**Methodology:**
- Start with one specific GTM objective (3-6 month window)
- Input outside view: competitor research, market data, industry benchmarks
- Input inside view: team insights and direct experience
- Generate actionable, testable ideas
- Prioritize using value vs. effort matrix

**On AI integration:** Use AI for objective prioritization with human oversight. "AI doesn't fall in love with its own ideas or push something just because it's really, really awesome."

**Deliverable:** A living roadmap document that teams continuously update — not static slides.

---

## Selection Logic

- No clear segment yet → **Market Problem Map**
- Clear segment but lacking traction → **GTM Blueprint**
- Uncertain overall strategy → **GTM Power Hour**

---

## Critical Counterpoint

Reader Sadik Shaikh: frameworks often aren't the limiting factor — "they fail because they spent too long following a framework that reality had already invalidated." The tension between systematic planning and market responsiveness is real.

This connects directly to motion decay: a framework is only useful as long as the assumptions underneath it still hold.
