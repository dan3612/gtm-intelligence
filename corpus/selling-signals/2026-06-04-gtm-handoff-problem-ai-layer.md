# Your GTM Team Has a Handoff Problem

**Source:** Selling Signals · Bianca Caballero · June 4, 2026

## Core Thesis

The vendors aren't trying to improve GTM handoffs anymore — they're trying to remove them.

OpenAI embedded ZoomInfo's GTM Context Graph into Codex. Apollo.io brought prospecting workflows into Perplexity. McKinsey's latest B2B research shows performance gaps widening between leaders and laggards. Different headlines, same direction.

The companies that win won't be the ones with the most aligned teams. They'll be the ones that need the fewest handoffs in the first place.

---

## Signal 1: GTM Knowledge Is Moving Into the AI Layer

OpenAI's integration with ZoomInfo puts company intelligence, buying signals, and account context inside the workspace where people are already working — before they ask for it.

For years GTM was built around finding information. Reps researched accounts. Marketers built segments. RevOps connected systems. Everyone spent time hunting for context before making a decision.

**Now the context shows up first.** Gathering information becomes less important. Knowing what to do with it becomes much more important.

> "The rep who wins won't be the one who spent three hours researching an account; it'll be the one who asks better questions once the research is already done."

**RevOps implication:** Prioritize data quality over dashboard quantity. Bad CRM data won't disappear in an AI-first workflow. It becomes everyone's problem faster.

**Marketing implication:** Make positioning, use cases, and customer proof easy for AI systems to understand and surface. If your differentiation only exists in a slide deck, you're already losing visibility.

---

## Signal 2: Fastest-Growing Companies Are Deleting Layers of Work

McKinsey's B2B growth research: leaders are pulling away not because of productivity gains but because they're building operating models with less friction between insight and execution.

Most companies are still approaching AI as a productivity project:
- Can we write content faster?
- Can we automate reporting?
- Can we save a few hours each week?

Growth leaders are asking a more uncomfortable question: **Which parts of our process should exist at all?**

The marketing roles under pressure aren't disappearing because AI writes better copy. They're disappearing because organizations built layers of coordination, reporting, routing, and administrative work that AI can increasingly absorb.

> "Every layer between customer insight and action introduces delay. The companies growing fastest are removing those layers."

**RevOps implication:** Document decision logic before introducing agents and automation. AI amplifies existing systems — good ones and bad ones.

---

## Signal 3: Prospecting Is Becoming a Conversation

Apollo.io's integration with Perplexity: execute prospecting workflows through natural-language conversation. Instead of opening five tools to research an account, build a list, identify contacts, and prioritize outreach — users start with a question and the system assembles the workflow behind the scenes.

The outcome isn't fewer tools. It's fewer reasons to think about them.

> "Your competitive advantage won't come from having more tools; it'll come from having cleaner systems feeding the AI layer."

**RevOps implication:** Identify workflows requiring three or more platforms. Prime candidates for consolidation over the next two years.

---

## Bottom Line

Most GTM teams are organized around moving information. Marketing gathers it, sales translates it, RevOps routes it, managers review it. Then everyone schedules a meeting about it.

If the vendors are right — and the trend data suggests they are — the winning companies won't out-coordinate their competitors. They'll out-simplify them.
