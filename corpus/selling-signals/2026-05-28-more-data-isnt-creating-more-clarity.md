# More Data Isn't Creating More Clarity

**Source:** Selling Signals (TechnologyAdvice) · Bianca Caballero · May 28, 2026

## Core Thesis

Modern GTM teams are not struggling because they lack information. They're struggling because interpretation is fragmenting faster than organizations can coordinate around it. The competitive advantage has shifted from generating more signals to developing shared organizational judgment about what those signals mean.

## Three Critical Signals

### 1. AI Adoption Outpacing Alignment
Microsoft and LinkedIn research shows teams are rapidly deploying AI tools independently, creating fragmented buyer interpretations across functions. Leadership expects faster output and faster decision-making, but without coordination, each function draws different conclusions from the same data.

### 2. Compressed Buyer Discovery
Adobe's traffic report shows AI-referred visitors arrive with stronger engagement, lower bounce rates, and improving conversion behavior. Buyers are completing much of their discovery process before they ever enter an observable funnel stage — meaning traditional top-of-funnel signals are losing predictive value.

### 3. Alignment Over Volume
Fullcast and Pavilion benchmarks show efficient growth is driven by system alignment, not activity volume. Pipeline quality depends on cross-functional coordination rather than raw activity. Teams doing more outreach, more demos, more meetings — without alignment — are not outperforming teams that have fewer but better-coordinated touchpoints.

## Implications for RevOps

- Signal volume is no longer the constraint — signal interpretation is
- If Marketing, Sales, and CS are each reading signals through their own lens, the CRM becomes a collection of contradictory assumptions
- The organizations winning in 2026 are building shared judgment infrastructure: standardized signal definitions, cross-functional review cadences, and AI tools that synthesize rather than just surface
- Pre-funnel buyer behavior (AI-referred traffic, dark social, community engagement) must be incorporated into ICP and scoring models

## Bottom Line

More data without a shared interpretation layer creates coordination debt. The next RevOps priority isn't adding more signals — it's building the infrastructure that ensures every function draws the same conclusions from the signals already in the system.
